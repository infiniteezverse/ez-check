EZ CHECK · live demo

AI agents can ask.
Only your fingerprint can say yes.

When an AI agent tries something risky, like refunding a payment, EZ CHECK pauses it until you approve with your device passkey (Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello). Your signature covers the exact action you saw, so it can't be swapped afterwards.

1 Create your passkey

Your device makes a key pair. The private half never leaves your device and only signs when you use your fingerprint, face or PIN. Nothing is stored on our server.

2 Let the agent work

Pick what the agent tries to do. These are pretend Stripe charges; no real money moves.
